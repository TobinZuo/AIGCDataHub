# Licensing and rights

An AIGC dataset can have several independent rights layers:

1. repository code and download tooling;
2. metadata, URLs, captions, and annotations;
3. original image, video, or audio assets;
4. model-generated captions or derived annotations;
5. contractual platform terms and access restrictions;
6. privacy, publicity, trademark, and database rights.

The dataset card records metadata and media terms separately. `commercial_use`
and `redistribution` are operational review states, not legal conclusions.

## Minimum review questions

- Does the cited license apply to metadata, media, or both?
- Are source-level license and attribution fields preserved per item?
- Do platform terms permit automated acquisition and the intended use?
- Can a sample be traced, removed, and propagated to derived datasets?
- Does a hosted copy have authority to redistribute the media?
- Are generated assets subject to model-provider or source-asset terms?

When evidence is missing, use `unknown` or `review-required`; do not infer broad
rights from public accessibility or from an open-source code license.
