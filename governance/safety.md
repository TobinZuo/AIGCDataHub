# Safety

Safety filtering is a layered process, not one classifier invocation. At
minimum, define policies and measurements for sexual content, minors, graphic
violence, hate, illegal content, self-harm, and context-specific risks.

A defensible pipeline combines source restrictions, known-hash lists where
legally available, model-based screening, targeted human review, and post-release
reporting/takedown paths. Record classifier versions and thresholds, quarantine
suspected illegal material, and avoid exposing unsafe samples in debug artifacts.

Dataset-level safety claims must include sampling methodology and residual-risk
limitations. “Filtered” without methods, versions, and dates is not verifiable.
