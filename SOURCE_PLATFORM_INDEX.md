# Candidate content-source platform index

This registry keeps potential acquisition surfaces separate from released datasets. It does not provide a dataset download link or imply permission to crawl, train on, redistribute, or commercialize platform content.

- Platforms: 16
- Catalog boundary: Candidate content-source platforms are not dataset releases and do not provide a repository download action.
- Rights boundary: Every platform requires source-specific review of platform terms, rightsholder permissions, privacy, regional access, and the intended training use before collection.
- Publication boundary: Operational assessments from non-public planning material are intentionally excluded from the public registry.
